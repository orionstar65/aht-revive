OUR PROCESS

One simple process.
Every step visible.

We built a case-first workflow so that customers understand the diagnosis, the limitations, and the next action.

01
START / FREE

Open your recovery case

Register, describe the device model and symptoms, and provide an ownership reference. We store a fingerprint and last four characters of the serial number—not the full serial in your case record.

02
REVIEW / MANUAL

Verify ownership and scope

Our team checks whether you're authorized to request support and what recovery path applies. Enrollment release depends on the responsible organization or authorized seller.

03
CHOOSE / OPTIONAL

Request a deeper assessment

First run the free, read-only Mac information checker. It displays the serial, model and operating system locally, then verifies case eligibility and paid status through an authenticated request. Paid services use Stripe Checkout; we trust only verified payment webhooks.

04
FOLLOW THROUGH

Work through the plan

Once authorized and paid, download a separate diagnostic support tool. The server enforces case ownership and payment status before releasing it, and checks authorization again at execution. The tools never change enrollment settings.
