QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

The important stuff,
up front.

We believe the limitations should be as clear as the possibilities.

Can you permanently remove an organization's MDM enrollment? +

We don't guarantee MDM removal. Permanent release from Apple Business Manager generally requires action by the organization or another authorized party. Local workarounds do not change server-side ownership.

Do you support M4 and newer Apple Silicon Macs? +

We accept assessment requests for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Compatibility with specific procedures is reviewed case by case; we don't make universal bypass claims.

Can I check my Mac before paying? +

Yes. The free identification script displays Mac information locally. When you choose to send the details, it uses a short-lived case access code to check eligibility and payment status without charging you.

Is the advanced script available publicly? +

No. The separate advanced diagnostic tool is only downloadable to authenticated customers with verified ownership and a confirmed payment. It can be inspected by its recipient; local shell code cannot be hidden from someone who runs it.

Why do I have to show proof of ownership? +

Mac recovery can involve sensitive account and organizational restrictions. Ownership screening helps prevent unauthorized access and ensures we recommend the right support pathway.

What happens when I open a free case? +

You receive a private workspace to document the problem and track an initial ownership review. Any optional paid assessment is shown separately after verification.

Will the service erase my Mac? +

Opening a case does not change your Mac. Both Mac scripts are customer-run and read-only; the website cannot remotely execute them. Recovery procedures with data-loss risks are discussed separately.

Are payments refundable? +

See our terms for the refund process. We aim to resolve billing concerns fairly and make the scope of paid technical review clear before checkout.
