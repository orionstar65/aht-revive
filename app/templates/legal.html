Information we collect

We collect account name, email, secure password hash, submitted issue descriptions, ownership references, device model, OS, processor, and a one-way serial fingerprint with the last four serial characters. We also store support conversations, order status, and operational audit events.

How we use it

We use this information to operate accounts, verify authorization, provide technical assessments, respond to support cases, prevent misuse, and keep payment records. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store payment card details.

Retention and security

Data is retained while required for support, accounting, and legitimate business purposes. Production operators must establish retention schedules, access controls, encrypted backups, and a data deletion process before launch.

Third parties and contact

Stripe processes checkout data when configured. Hosting and email providers may process operational information. For privacy requests, contact the business through its published support channel.

{% else %}

Scope of service

AHT Revive provides diagnostics, documentation, and technical assistance for devices that customers own or are authorized to service. We do not guarantee removal of Activation Lock, organizational enrollment, or other access restrictions.

Ownership authorization

Customers must provide accurate device information and affirm that they own or are explicitly authorized to request assistance for each device. We may decline requests that cannot be adequately verified.

Payments and refunds

Initial intake is free. Paid diagnostic and specialist services are optional and have published prices. A service fee covers review and support, not a guaranteed outcome. Refund requests should be assessed against applicable laws and the portion of the service already performed. The final commercial policy requires legal review and a published support email before launch.

Data-loss warning

Recovery, reinstall, and account changes can lead to data loss. Customers should maintain backups and review the consequences before taking technical actions.

Independent service

AHT Revive is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc. Apple, Mac, and macOS are trademarks of their respective owners.
